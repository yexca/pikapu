Pikapu v0.1.1 refreshes the reading layout: a shared header and toolbar for
both layouts, a quick way to open articles in a new tab, and a mascot who
keeps you company across the app.

## Reading

- A header spans the top of both layouts: the current view and its unread
  count on the left; the last-update time, Refresh, the layout switch, the
  theme menu, and Settings on the right. These moved from the sidebar footer
  and the list header.
- Search, Unread / All, Mark all as read, and the feed's ⋯ menu sit in one
  toolbar below the header, in the same order in both layouts; in the hub it
  lines up with the stream.
- Refresh in the header refreshes the open feed, or all feeds from other
  views, like the `R` shortcut. The sidebar's separate Refresh all button is
  gone.
- In the classic layout, each article in the list has a button at its top
  right that opens the original page in a new browser tab without selecting
  it; with "Mark articles as read when opened" on, it also marks the article
  as read.
- Before the first subscription, the list shows "No articles yet" instead of
  "All caught up".

## Look and Feel

- New app icon and a mascot, a messenger mage who delivers feeds.
- She appears on the sign-in and setup page, in the empty classic reader
  (pointing at the list), at the end of each article, and in empty states:
  all caught up, no search results, nothing starred, and no articles yet.
- When the app or an article fails to load, she apologizes instead of a
  greyed-out logo.

## Operations

- Images on Docker Hub (`yexca/pikapu`) and the GitHub Container Registry
  (`ghcr.io/yexca/pikapu`) are tagged `0.1.1`, `0.1`, and `latest`.
